TEST-DAY PLAN

48 hours out

  • Last real session is behind you. Easy movement only.
  • Eat normally. Hydrate steadily — don't try to catch up the morning of.
  • Lay out everything: workout gear, watch, water, snack.

Night before

  • Normal dinner, nothing experimental.
  • Set two alarms. Screens off 30 minutes before bed.
  • Review your pacing plan once, then let it go.

Morning of

  • Wake 2.5–3 hours before the test. Eat a familiar breakfast.
  • Water early, then sip. Bathroom stops planned, not panicked.
  • Arrive early. Own your warm-up space.

Warm-up (20 min)

  • 5 min easy row — conversational pace.
  • Dynamic moves: leg swings, arm circles, inchworms, bodyweight squats.
  • 2 × 10 push-ups at test cadence. 2 × 0:30 plank. 3 × 10-stroke row pickups.

Event order & pacing

  • Push-ups (2:00): steady cadence from the first rep. Don't sprint the first 30 seconds — that's where tests are lost.
  • Plank: one long effort. Breathe slow, hips level, eyes down.
  • 2,000 m row: controlled start, build through the second 500, push the third 500, empty the tank in the last 500.

After

  • Easy 10 min row or walk to cool down. Log your scores immediately while fresh.
  • 270 is the mission. Whatever the number — you did the twelve weeks.
